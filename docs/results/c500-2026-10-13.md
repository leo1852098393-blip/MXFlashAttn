The Qwen3-4B long-context run peaked at approximately 59.6 GiB. These
measurements are workload-specific and must not be generalized to every model,
batch size, or context length. This round did not produce a unified TTFT/P95
measurement.

## Guard behavior

The M1 binding contract is `GQ in {2, 4, 8}` and `head_dim in {64, 128}`.
Unsupported shapes must not enter the M1 binding directly. In the vLLM
official-split path, `head_dim=256` is returned to the original MetaX/vendor
KV-cache implementation. The Qwen3.5-9B guardfix generation completed
successfully with zero M1 dispatches and 4/4 text matches for both UM modes.

## Evidence rules

- `m1_split_decode` is required before attributing a performance result to M1.
- `native_decode` is the v7/native comparison path.
- `vendor_direct` is the MetaX/vendor path and is not attributed to M1.
- Continuous-batching text matches are not a substitute for fixed-batch
  token-id correctness tests.

The 2026-10-07 Gate 4 report remains available as historical development
provenance. Its higher historical results, such as 3.67x and 6.48x, are not
mixed with this 2026-10-13 retest.
